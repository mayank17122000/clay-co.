Returns & Damage Policy

Because ceramics are fragile and each product is handled and packed individually, Clay & Co. accepts return requests only when an item is received broken or damaged in transit.

When a return is eligible

A return request is eligible only if the item is broken, cracked or materially damaged when you receive it. Returns for change of mind, preference, colour variation, size expectation or other non-damage reasons are not accepted.

Same-day request required

You must report the damage and submit your return request on the same day the parcel is delivered. Requests made after the delivery day may not be accepted because we may no longer be able to verify that the damage occurred during transit.

Evidence required

Please provide clear photographs of the damaged product, the outer packaging, inner protective packaging and the shipping label. A short unboxing video is strongly recommended where available, especially for visibly damaged parcels.

Keep all packaging

Do not discard the box, cushioning, labels or damaged item until your claim has been reviewed. We may need the original packaging to verify the claim or to arrange collection, if collection is available.

How to report damage

Contact us through the website Contact section or WhatsApp on the delivery day. Include your name, order reference, product name and the requested photographs or video so the issue can be reviewed without unnecessary delay.

Review of the request

Once the information is received, Clay & Co. will review the claim. Approval is subject to the submitted evidence showing that the item arrived damaged. We may request additional photographs or information if required.

Resolution

For an approved damage claim, the available resolution may be a replacement, exchange or refund depending on product availability, the nature of the damage and the circumstances of the order. We will communicate the applicable option after reviewing the claim.

Natural variations

Minor differences in shade, glaze, texture, surface pattern or finish that are part of the character of ceramic products are not considered transit damage. Screen colours and photography may also vary slightly from the item viewed in person.

Items damaged after delivery

Damage caused after delivery by dropping, impact, misuse, improper cleaning, storage or handling is not eligible under this policy. The same-day damage policy is intended specifically for items that arrive broken or damaged during shipping.

Important: If your parcel arrives damaged, contact us on the same day and keep the complete packaging. This gives us the best chance to verify and resolve the issue fairly.

Return eligibility: damaged or broken on arrival only
